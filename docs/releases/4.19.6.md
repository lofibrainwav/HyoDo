# HyoDo 4.19.6 Release Notes

## Intent

4.19.6 clarifies HyoDo's public purpose as an open, model-agnostic evidence
and verification layer, aligns the website and release guidance with that
purpose, and records the legacy score as compatibility behavior. It also
improves dashboard startup reporting and test-gate skip visibility.

## Scope

- The site asks what proves an AI completion claim and explains that HyoDo
  reports evidence while the integrating host retains action authorization.
- The six reference values are not presented as one canonical score. The
  existing five-input geometric-mean score remains available for compatibility
  while its replacement is being updated; `HYOGOOK V5` is explained as the
  internal name of that older calculation.
- Dashboard startup binds its loopback listener before initial evidence
  collection and reports explicit `starting`, `failed`, and `UNOBSERVED` states.
- Test-gate messages include pytest skip and expected-failure reasons.
