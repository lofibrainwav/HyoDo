# Evidence Plate v1

`hyodo.evidence-plate/v1` is the measurement surface for shared evidence
atoms projected through one lens. It is deliberately not a score, decision,
approval, route, or execution permission.

Every observed plate is bound to `exact_artifact_sha`. Missing or malformed
artifact identity remains `UNOBSERVED`. Authority-shaped input fields are
excluded from atom extraction and cannot be promoted by this module.

The six lens names are the public v1 vocabulary; this runtime only extracts
evidence and preserves residuals. It does not claim that an observation is
good, safe, mergeable, or authorized.
