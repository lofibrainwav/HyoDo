# M1 Controlled Factorial Benchmark Harness

M1 is a deterministic measurement harness, not a quality claim. It generates
10 controlled factor scenarios with 10 replicates each, for exactly 100 cases.

The harness checks that missing artifact identity, provenance, or relevant
evidence remains `UNOBSERVED`. Authority-shaped and cross-lens fields are
recorded as test dimensions but do not become decisions or scores.

The result is a candidate research artifact. It requires later independent
verification and does not authorize routing, merge, deployment, or publication.
